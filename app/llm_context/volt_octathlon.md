# Volt Octathlon

## Training Goal
Build the qualities the Volt Octathlon demands: full-body muscular endurance, rowing and skiing engine, assault bike power, and the ability to maintain technique under accumulated fatigue across 8 stations. Training sessions develop these qualities — they are not mini copies of the race.

**Race format for reference:** 8 stations performed back-to-back, no rest between stations. Each station has a creative name ending in "8":

| Station | Name       | Movement                   | Load / Distance    |
|---------|------------|----------------------------|--------------------|
| 1       | Initi8     | Thrusters                  | 2 × 10kg DB       |
| 2       | Elev8      | Row                        | 1000m              |
| 3       | Stimul8    | Slams                      | 10kg               |
| 4       | Acceler8   | Ski                        | 1000m              |
| 5       | Gravit8    | KB Swing                   | 20kg               |
| 6       | Domin8     | Assault Bike               | 50 cal             |
| 7       | Anihil8    | Devil Press                | 2 × 10kg DB       |
| 8       | Termin8    | Run                        | 1000m              |

Training sessions are NOT race simulations — they build the physical qualities the race tests.

## Anchor Movements
The prompt specifies 2–4 Octathlon movements for this session. Build those into the main set as the primary training focus. Race-accurate weights and distances for the selected movements are provided in the prompt.

## Primary Exercises — Competition Movements (use often)
These are the actual Volt Octathlon race stations. They should form the backbone of every session alongside the anchor movements.

Thrusters (2 × 10kg DB), Rowing (1000m), Slams (10kg), SkiErg (1000m), KB Swings (20kg), Assault Bike (50 cal), Devil Press (2 × 10kg DB), Running (1000m)

## Exercise Variations — Training Alternatives
Not every session needs the exact race version. Swap in these variations freely to add variety while training the same movement patterns and energy systems.

**Thruster variations** (swap for Thrusters):
Barbell Thrusters, KB Thrusters, Squat to Press, Push Press, Goblet Squat to Press, DB Clusters

**Slam variations** (swap for Slams):
Wall Balls, Med Ball Clean and Press, Med Ball Over Shoulder, Overhead Slam to Burpee

**KB Swing variations** (swap for KB Swings):
KB Clean and Press, KB Snatch, KB High Pull, American KB Swing, Alternating KB Swing, Double KB Swing

**Devil Press variations** (swap for Devil Press):
Dumbbell Burpees, DB Snatch, Man Makers, Burpee to DB Clean and Press

## Secondary Exercises — Supplementary Training (use sparingly)
These develop the same physical qualities but are NOT competition movements. Use 2–3 per session to add variety — they should never outnumber or overshadow the primary exercises.

**Posterior chain and power**
Romanian Deadlift, Hip Thrust, Box Jumps, Good Morning, Step-ups (heavy)

**Pressing and upper body**
Push Press, DB Bench Press, Push-ups, Dips, Shoulder Press

**Pulling and grip**
Ring Rows, Lat Pulldown, DB Row, Cable Row, Pull-ups

**Legs and lunges**
Goblet Squat, Walking Lunges, Reverse Lunges (loaded), Jump Squats, Leg Press

**Core and stability**
Sit-ups, V-ups, Hollow Body, Dead Bug, Plank

## Session Structure
Warm-up → Main set (anchor movements + primary/secondary exercises in varied formats) → Optional finisher → Cool-down stretches.

## Coaching Notes
- The Octathlon is a continuous effort with no rest between stations — train transitions and the ability to work under fatigue.
- Pair machine work (row, ski, bike) with strength movements (thrusters, swings, slams) to simulate the race's alternating pattern.
- Devil Press and Thrusters are the most demanding stations — give them extra training volume.
- The 1000m row and 1000m ski should be trained at sustainable race pace, not all-out sprints.
- 50 cal on the bike is a grind — train bike efforts at both sprint and sustained paces.
- The 1000m run comes last when athletes are most fatigued — train running under fatigue by placing it after heavy conditioning.
